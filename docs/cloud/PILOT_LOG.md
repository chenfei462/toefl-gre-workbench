## Completion criteria

- [ ] Seven actual dated learner sessions, with retained study data and no unexplained loss.
- [ ] Phone and computer consistency checked on real devices.
- [ ] Due errors return and vocabulary due dates are reviewed for usefulness.
- [ ] Priority and Daily Prescription changes are observed against actual evidence.
- [ ] A mock/checkpoint's influence on future plans is recorded.
- [ ] Recommendation feedback and dated backups are retained.
- [ ] Incidents are resolved or explicitly remain open before algorithm calibration.

Collect evidence before changing mastery/priority weights. Do not infer calibration or score improvement from the automated gates. The app provides planning and tracking, not official ETS scoring. See [OPERATIONS_RUNBOOK.md](OPERATIONS_RUNBOOK.md) for safe failure handling.
